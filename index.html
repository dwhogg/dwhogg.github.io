hermes-mail

A private, personal email assistant. It reads its owner's own mailboxes with read-only access and files the messages that matter into their personal notes.

Home Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What it is

hermes-mail is a self-hosted tool built and run by one person for their own use. It is not a public service, it is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's own Gmail account using Google's read-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly).
  • Saves messages with chosen labels, such as receipts, as Markdown notes in the owner's private notes.
  • Produces summaries and highlights important, time-sensitive or suspicious messages for the owner.

What it never does

  • It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • It does not sell, share or advertise with anyone's data.
  • It does not access any account other than the owner's own.
